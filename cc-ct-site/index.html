The CC/CT Project

Continuous Capture / Continuous Training

The seam. Capture ends when a clean, redacted event lands in a sink you control. Training begins with deciding which 2–5% of it is worth learning from. The boundary is the canonical event schema event.v1 — a technical fault line, not a commercial one, which is why it holds.

Overall product — the loop

Apps call the gateway; Capture taps it off the request path; the customer's own log shipper carries event.v1 across the seam; Training refines and trains; the gate releases weights back.

Deployed models generate new traffic, which becomes the next dataset version. That arrow is what makes this a subscription rather than a project.

Continuous Capture — open source

Taps an existing gateway off the request path, assembles conversations, redacts inside your network, and joins outcome signals that arrive after the completion.

Writes JSONL. Fluent Bit, Vector, or the OTel Collector do the shipping — we do not implement sinks at all, so we structurally cannot prefer our own destination.

Continuous Training — commercial

Refines bronze to gold by aggressive subtraction, mines preference pairs from edit signals, trains, and proves the result beats both the base model and the frontier baseline.

No weights leave without green evaluations and a zero canary-leak result. The gate is a pipeline stage, not a policy.

Why the split is here

Capture wins by being trusted and ubiquitous — a collector reading every employee prompt is exactly the software a CISO wants to read the source of.

Training wins by accumulated judgement about what to discard, which is the part that does not survive being copied.
